# Episode 2: The Camel That Cried

**Quality:** kindness to animals.
**Length:** about 4 minutes.
**Language of this draft:** English. An Urdu version follows once the English is approved.
**Source for the seerah section:** Sunan Abi Dawud 2549 (graded sahih), from Abdullah ibn Ja'far. Retold in new words; nothing is added to what happened.

**Status:** first draft, for the owner to read. Have someone knowledgeable check the seerah section before the episode is published.

## Who is in it

Abba, Nani, Zayn (4), Noor (6), Zara (3), Sami (2), and **Mano, the family cat** (new: she needs a character sheet before the pictures are made).

## Sound

No music anywhere. Natural sound only: birds, the hum of the city far below, palm leaves, a cat's meow, water pouring, the creak of the swing. In the seerah section: wind in the palms, a camel's low cry, insects in the evening.

---

## Part 1: Today (about 40 seconds)

**Place:** the open terrace, then the swing corner. A hot, bright afternoon.

1. *The open terrace. Zayn and Noor are kicking the yellow ball to each other, laughing. Sami sits on the grass watching. Zara runs after the ball, copying everything Noor does.*

2. *In the shade by the planter, Mano the cat lies flat on the cool tiles. She lifts her head and meows. A long, tired meow.*

3. *Sami points at Mano.*
   **SAMI:** Mano! Mano!
   **ZAYN** *(not looking, kicking the ball):* Not now, Sami.
   **ZARA** *(pointing the same way, copying):* Mano!

4. *Mano gets up, walks slowly to her bowl by the door, and sniffs it. It is empty and dry. She licks the bottom of it and meows again, louder.*

5. *Close on the empty bowl: a plain blue bowl, with no writing on it.*

6. *Abba, on the swing with Nani, has seen all of it. He calls gently.*
   **ABBA:** Zayn. Whose job was Mano's water this morning?
   **ZAYN** *(stops; the ball rolls away):* ...Mine. But she didn't *say* anything, Abba. Cats can't talk.
   **NOOR:** She was meowing all day.
   **ZAYN:** Meowing isn't *talking*.

7. *Abba pats the swing seat. The children come and settle on the grass in front of him. Sami climbs onto his lap. Nani smiles.*
   **ABBA:** You know... that reminds me of something that really happened. About an animal that couldn't talk either. But someone understood it anyway.

8. *The camera drifts up from the family to the sky over the city, and the bright afternoon slowly warms into the gold of evening.*

## Part 2: The story (about 2 minutes)

**Picture:** a palm garden in Madinah, long ago. No person is ever shown: not the Prophet ﷺ, not the boy, not the young man. Only the garden, the camel and the light. When he arrives, the camera is where he stands, and the camel looks at us.

Abba's voice tells the story over the pictures. The children's faces are seen twice, briefly, back on the terrace.

| # | Picture | Abba says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seerah shot 1: the palm garden from outside, the open gate, the camera moving through it. | Long, long ago, in the city of Madinah, the Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, went into a garden full of date palms. A boy called Abdullah was riding with him that day. He saw everything, and when he grew up, he told people about it. That's how we know. |
| 2 | Seerah shot 2: inside, a thin, tired camel standing alone under the palms, head hanging low. | In the garden, there was a camel. A thin, tired camel, all on its own. |
| 3 | Seerah shot 4: the camel lifts its head and looks straight at us, ears forward. | And when the camel saw the Prophet ﷺ coming... |
| 4 | Seerah shot 3: close on the camel's eye. A tear rolls down. | ...it cried. It made a sad, groaning sound, and tears ran from its eyes. |
| 5 | Back on the terrace: Zara's eyes go wide. Sami holds Abba's kurta. | |
| 6 | Seerah shot 5: the camel's head lowered, eyes closing, calm, as if someone gentle is beside it. No hand is seen. | So he went to the camel, and very gently, he stroked it behind its ears. And slowly, slowly, the camel grew quiet. |
| 7 | Seerah shot 2 again, wider: the garden, the camel standing calmer now. | Then he asked: "Whose camel is this?" And a young man came and said, "It's mine." |
| 8 | Seerah shot 6: an empty trough, a dry bucket, and a pile of heavy sacks with a worn rope. | And the Prophet ﷺ said to him: "Don't you fear Allah about this animal that Allah has given you? It has told me that you keep it hungry, and that you make it work too hard." |
| 9 | Back on the terrace: Zayn, very still, thinking. | |
| 10 | Seerah shot 8: sunset over the garden, the camel resting on folded legs under the palms, a few birds crossing the sky. | |

**Notes for the seerah section**

- The camel's cry is one low natural sound under picture 4. It never speaks.
- Pictures 7 and 8 carry the Prophet's ﷺ words, but no person is seen: only the garden, the trough and the sacks.
- The hadith does not say what the young man did afterwards, so the story stops where the hadith stops. The earlier shot list had a shot of the trough refilled and the camel eating. **It has been dropped**, because it would show something the source does not tell us.
- Picture 10 is a quiet ending, not an event: the evening garden, with no words over it.

## Part 3: Back to today (about 30 seconds)

**Place:** the swing corner, then the door to the living room. Evening light.

1. *The sky over the city fades back from the garden's gold to the terrace in late afternoon. The children sit quietly for a moment.*
   **ZAYN** *(slowly):* The camel told him... without words.
   **ABBA:** Hmm. And how did it tell him?
   **NOOR:** It cried.
   **ZAYN:** ...And Mano meowed.

2. *Zayn jumps up and runs to Mano's bowl. Sami slides off Abba's lap and toddles after him. Zara runs too, copying.*

3. *Zayn fills the bowl from a jug. He is careful, using both hands. Sami "helps" by holding the jug's handle. Water splashes a little.*

4. *Mano comes at once and drinks and drinks. Zayn sits on his heels beside her and watches.*
   **ZAYN** *(softly):* Sorry, Mano. I'm listening now.

5. *Nani, from the swing, says it in passing, to Abba, not to the camera:*
   **NANI:** Animals can't use words. So we have to use our eyes.

6. *Mano, finished, rubs against Zayn's knee and purrs. Zayn grins up at Noor.*

7. **Hint of the next episode:** *As the picture fades, Zayn and Noor start a new game. In the background, Sami begins to cry. Noor glances at him... and the picture fades out before we see what she does.*
