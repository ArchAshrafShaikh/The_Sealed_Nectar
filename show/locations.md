# Location sheet

Every place the show's present-day scenes happen, with the prompt to draw each one. The home is Abba and Dadi's: a garden terrace on top of a high-rise in a busy city.

## How to use this sheet

1. **Make location 1 first and choose the version you like.** It is the master picture: it fixes the style for everything else.
2. **For every other location, attach the master picture as the reference image** and add the line "Match the illustration style, colours and line quality of the reference image exactly."
3. Model: Nano Banana Pro. Size: 16:9, 2K.
4. **Every location is drawn empty.** No people or animals. The family is added later.
5. Reject any picture with writing, letters or symbols in it, or that looks like a photo or 3D render.

## The style block

Paste this at the start of every prompt.

```
Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, soft gouache texture, simple rounded shapes, clean and uncluttered, warm gentle colours. Wide 16:9 view. No people, no animals. No text, no writing, no letters, no symbols. Not photorealistic, not 3D.
```

## Colours that stay the same everywhere

| Thing | Colour |
|---|---|
| The swing's frame, the climbing bars | Bright warm yellow |
| The swing seat, the sofas | Teal green |
| Lawn | Bright fresh green |
| Terrace floor | Grey and white zigzag tiles |
| Walls and pillars | Soft warm grey and cream |
| The rug on the swing | Deep red with a pale pattern |
| City beyond | Pale, hazy blue-grey towers |

Yellow, teal and green are the show's three colours. The red rug is the one accent.

## Things that are always in the same place

- The swing stands on the lawn with the planter and the city behind it and to its right.
- Two chairs face the swing: a grey rocking chair and a small dark brown chair.
- The climbing bars are a few steps to the right of the swing.
- The living room door opens straight onto the swing corner.
- The terrace has a simple glass railing, never netting.

## 1. The swing corner (master picture)

Where every story begins. Abba sits here with the children.

```
Soft morning sunlight. A rooftop garden terrace high above a big modern city.

In the centre stands a garden swing bench: a bright yellow metal frame with curved legs that flare outward at the bottom, a curved tan canopy roof on top, a teal-green slatted bench seat for three hanging from the frame, a round green ring decorating each side of the frame, and a red patterned rug draped over the backrest.

The swing stands on a bright green lawn. To the left the floor changes to grey and white zigzag patterned tiles. Behind the swing is a pale cream wall with plain wooden cupboard doors. To the right is a low stone planter filled with palms, a plant with dark red leaves and leafy green shrubs. Beyond the planter, a simple glass railing and a soft hazy skyline of tall city towers under a pale blue sky.

In the right foreground, a grey rocking chair and a small dark brown chair face the swing. At the far right edge, part of a yellow and green climbing frame. No netting.
```

**Versions to make from the master** (attach it as the reference, and change only the first sentence and the sky):

| Version | Replace the first sentence with |
|---|---|
| Sunset | Warm orange and pink sunset light, long soft shadows, the sky glowing orange behind the city towers. |
| Night | Night time, a deep blue sky full of stars and a crescent moon, small warm lights in the distant city towers, one soft lamp glowing near the swing. |
| Dawn | Early dawn, a pale pink and gold sky, cool soft light, the city still quiet. |

The night and dawn versions matter most: the starry sky and the sunrise are how the show moves from the terrace into the seerah stories.

## 2. The playground

Where the children play, share, take turns and fall out.

```
Soft morning sunlight. A children's play area on a rooftop garden terrace high above a big modern city, under wide pale concrete beams held up by big square grey pillars.

On a bright green lawn stands a climbing frame made of yellow and green metal bars: a tall ladder of zigzag green rungs on a yellow post, and a row of overhead monkey bars with a hanging yellow swing seat and two hanging rings. Beside it, a small white and yellow toddler slide. A yellow and black football and a yellow frisbee lie on the grass.

Behind the play area, a low stone planter full of leafy green shrubs with a few red flowers. Past the lawn, grey and white zigzag patterned tiles lead to a simple glass railing and a soft hazy skyline of tall city towers. No netting.
```

## 3. The open terrace and the city view

For the long looks at the sky: sunset, stars, sunrise. Also for running about and ball games.

```
Soft morning sunlight. A wide open rooftop terrace high above a big modern city. The floor is grey and white zigzag patterned tiles with a strip of bright green lawn along the edge. Two big square grey pillars hold up pale concrete beams overhead. A low stone bench runs along the edge of the terrace, with a simple glass railing above it.

Beyond the railing, a wide view over a huge busy city: many tall towers fading into soft blue haze, with a big open sky taking up the top half of the picture. A yellow football rests on the lawn. No netting.
```

Make sunset, night and dawn versions of this one too, using the same replacement sentences as location 1.

## 4. The living room

For indoor moments: rainy days, guests, quiet time.

```
Soft daylight through a big window. A calm modern living room. On the left wall, a very tall wide window made of many square panes, with long grey curtains tied back at each side, looking out over a hazy city skyline. Sunlight falls through the panes and makes a pattern of squares on a shiny pale tiled floor.

Along the right wall, a long L-shaped sofa in teal green with soft vertical ribbing and matching cushions, and a second smaller teal sofa in the foreground. Between them, a low dark coffee table on a brown shaggy rug. Above the sofa, four black cone-shaped lamps hang in a row at different heights. On the grey wall hang two framed pictures of simple geometric star patterns. A ceiling fan above.
```

The frames show geometric patterns only. Never ask for calligraphy: image models garble Arabic writing.

## 5. The doorway to the garden

The shot that links inside and outside: the children run out through it, Abba is seen on the swing beyond.

```
Soft daylight. Inside a calm modern living room, looking toward a tall open door in a grey wall. Through the doorway, a rooftop garden is visible: a bright green lawn, grey and white zigzag tiles, a garden swing bench with a bright yellow frame, a curved tan canopy and a teal-green seat, palms behind it and a hazy city skyline.

Inside the room, on the left, a small teal-green ribbed sofa and a dark wooden bookshelf with a desk and a green chair. A small flower-shaped doormat lies in front of the door on a shiny pale tiled floor.
```

## 6. The dining table

For meals: thankfulness, sharing food, the complaint about dinner.

```
Warm evening light. A cosy dining corner in a modern home. A dark wooden dining table with six chairs, set with simple plates, a water jug and a bowl of fruit. Behind the table, a wide sliding glass door stands open onto a rooftop garden with a green lawn and grey and white zigzag tiles, and the evening sky beyond. A soft lamp hangs above the table. A baby's high chair in pale blue stands at one corner of the table.
```

## 7. The children's bedroom

Not from a photo. For night-time episodes: the thunderstorm, bedtime questions.

```
Night time, soft warm light from a small bedside lamp. A cosy children's bedroom. Two small beds with teal and yellow blankets, a low bookshelf with picture books and a few toys, a round rug on the floor. A big window with open curtains shows a deep blue night sky with stars and the small warm lights of distant city towers.
```

For the thunderstorm episode, make a second version: replace the sky with "dark storm clouds and rain running down the window glass".

## 8. The building from outside

Not from a photo. The opening shot of an episode: where we are.

```
Soft morning sunlight. A tall modern apartment tower seen from below and to one side, rising above a busy city of smaller buildings and tree-lined streets. At the very top of the tower, a rooftop terrace overflows with green plants and palms. Pale blue sky with a few soft clouds. Other towers fade into soft haze in the distance.
```

## 9. The lobby

Not from a photo. Where the children meet other people: neighbours, the guard, a new family.

```
Bright daylight. The entrance lobby of a modern apartment building. A shiny pale stone floor, a wooden reception desk with a small plant on it, two teal-green armchairs and a low table, big potted palms, and tall glass entrance doors with a busy sunny street visible outside. On the back wall, a pair of silver lift doors with a small glowing button beside them.
```

## 10. Inside the lift

Not from a photo. A small space where two people must share: greeting a neighbour, making room, holding the door.

```
Soft indoor light. The inside of a clean modern lift seen from the back, looking toward closed silver doors. Warm wooden panels on the side walls, a handrail, a row of small round glowing buttons with no numbers or writing on them, and a soft light in the ceiling.
```

## Order to make them in

1. Location 1, day. Choose the master.
2. Location 1 at night, as the test that the reference keeps the swing identical.
3. Locations 2 and 3, since most episodes use them.
4. The rest as episodes need them. The pilot (the camel episode) needs 1, 2 and 3 only.
