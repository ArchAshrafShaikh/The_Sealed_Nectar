# Sealed Nectar Stories

Stories from the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for young children, as a story app and as short watchable episodes.

**Read `HANDOVER.md` first.** It has the current direction, what exists, what is unchecked, and the plan for the pilot episode.

## Rules that always apply

- Never show a person: no figures, faces, hands, shadows or silhouettes of people, in drawings, generated pictures or video. Places, animals and objects only.
- No music. Voice and natural sound only.
- Retell in new words and keep to the sources; do not invent events or dialogue. Use honorifics.
- Show the owner one example before producing many, and ask before spending his Higgsfield credits.
- Write to the owner in plain language; he follows from a tablet.
- After changing any site file, raise `VERSION` in `sw.js`.
- Commit and push to `claude/loving-darwin-sz5611`. No pull request unless asked.
